Our Story

Built by people who hated the dentist.

SmileCraft began in 2009 when our founding dentist — a self-confessed nervous patient — decided to build the clinic she had always wanted to find. Warm lighting instead of fluorescent glare. Appointments that start on time. Explanations in plain words, with the price agreed before any work begins.

Word spread. Two chairs became six, then a dedicated pediatric wing, then a digital lab that mills crowns while you wait. Today our team of four specialists and eleven support staff cares for more than 12,000 patients — and the founding rule has never changed: treat every patient like a nervous first-timer, because comfort is the whole point.

What We Stand For

Four promises, kept daily.

Gentle First

Pain-free techniques and sedation options are standard here, not upgrades. Comfort comes before everything.

Honest Pricing

Written quotes before treatment, no hidden fees, and we will always mention the cheaper option if it fits.

Everyone Welcome

Milestones

Fifteen years of steady growth.

We never chased rapid expansion. Each step below was taken only when our patients asked for it — and only when we could do it well.
